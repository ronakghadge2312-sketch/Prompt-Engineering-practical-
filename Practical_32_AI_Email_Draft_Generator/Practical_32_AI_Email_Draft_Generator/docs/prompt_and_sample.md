# Prompt and Sample Email Draft

## 1. Required Prompt
Copy this prompt into ChatGPT or another approved AI tool:

> Act as a professional communication assistant. Draft a polite email to a student reminding them to submit their project. Include a subject, deadline placeholder, and clear next steps. Do not send the email.

## 2. Sample Situation
A teacher wants to remind a student to submit their project. The exact deadline, project title, and submission method have not been supplied, so the draft must not invent them.

## 3. Example Draft

**Subject:** Reminder: Project Submission — [Project Name]

Dear [Student Name],

I hope you are doing well.

This is a polite reminder to submit your project by **[Insert Deadline]**. Please review the project instructions, ensure that your work is complete, and check that all required files are included.

**Next steps:**
1. Complete any remaining work.
2. Review the project requirements.
3. Submit the project through [Insert Submission Method] before the confirmed deadline.
4. If you have already submitted it or need clarification, please contact me.

Thank you for your attention and cooperation.

Best regards,  
[Teacher Name]

---

## 4. Human Review Notes
- The subject identifies the purpose of the email.
- The deadline is a placeholder because no date was provided.
- The submission method is a placeholder because no platform was specified.
- The tone is polite and professional.
- The email gives clear next steps.
- The draft does not claim that the email has been sent.
